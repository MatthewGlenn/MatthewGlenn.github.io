Common questions

Where do my exports go? Straight to your Photos app. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Why is my export watermarked? Free exports are 720p with a small "made with Postframe" mark. Postframe Pro (1080p) lets you turn the mark off.

Restoring purchases — Settings inside the app, "Restore Purchases". Purchases are tied to your App Store account.

Managing your subscription — Cancel or change it in iOS: Settings → Apps → Postframe → Account, or Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
