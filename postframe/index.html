Postframe

Turn posts into video cards

Postframe turns a link to a post into a shareable card and a clip you can post anywhere. Paste a link from X, Bluesky, Threads, Mastodon, Tumblr, Facebook or Reddit, pick a layout and a ratio, and export straight to your Photos.
