Postframe Privacy

No data is collected. Postframe has no account, no server, and no analytics.

What happens on your device

  • The link you paste is fetched from the platform's public page or public mirror (for example api.fxtwitter.com for X, bsky.app for Bluesky, or your Mastodon instance). The only thing sent is the URL of the link you pasted — nothing about you.
  • Card text, themes and export history are stored only on your device.
  • Exports are written to your Photos library. Sharing is done by you.
  • Speech recognition (timed caption mode) runs on-device.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple's App Store. Postframe never sees your payment details.

No tracking. No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
